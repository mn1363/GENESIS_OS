## Purpose

The Ethical Governance and Moral Axiom Constitution Layer (EG-MACL) defines the
normative decision framework of GENESIS OS, establishing the moral constraints,
ethical boundaries, and value-aligned governance rules that regulate all system
actions, reasoning, and evolution.

It is the “value spine” of the entire architecture.
