# ENGINEERING INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

The Engineering Intelligence Framework (EIF) defines how GENESIS OS
continuously evaluates, understands and improves the technical state of all
engineering artifacts across the platform.

This framework converts raw engineering signals (code, workflows, runtime
telemetry, decisions, and system behavior) into structured intelligence that
can be analyzed, optimized, and governed.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mission

Provide a deterministic, explainable and continuously evolving intelligence
layer that improves engineering quality, system reliability and development
efficiency while preserving full traceability and governance compliance.

Every intelligence object shall have a globally unique Intelligence ID.

All insights shall be derived from verifiable engineering evidence.

Rule 03

Evaluation metrics shall be reproducible for identical inputs.

Rule 04

Optimization suggestions shall remain traceable to analysis results.

All intelligence processes shall be fully auditable.
