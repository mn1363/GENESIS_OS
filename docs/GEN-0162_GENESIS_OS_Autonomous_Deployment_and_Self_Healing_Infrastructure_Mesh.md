## Purpose

The Autonomous Deployment and Self-Healing Infrastructure Mesh (AD-SHIM)
defines the operational infrastructure layer of GENESIS OS responsible for
deploying, scaling, repairing, and continuously stabilizing all system
components across distributed environments.

It ensures GENESIS OS remains operational under all conditions, including
failure, overload, corruption, or partial system collapse.
