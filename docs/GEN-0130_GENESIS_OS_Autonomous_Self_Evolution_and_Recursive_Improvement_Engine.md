The Autonomous Self-Evolution and Recursive Improvement Engine (ASE-RIE)
defines the highest-order adaptive mechanism of GENESIS OS responsible for
system-wide self-modification, optimization, and structural evolution.

It enables GENESIS OS to improve itself over time based on performance,
telemetry, inference outcomes, and system-wide feedback loops.
