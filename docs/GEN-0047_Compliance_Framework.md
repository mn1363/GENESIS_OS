# COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

The Compliance Framework establishes the governance architecture for ensuring
that all engineering activities, operational workflows, AI interactions and
software artifacts conform to organizational policies, regulatory
requirements and internal engineering standards.

Compliance is a continuous engineering process rather than a final validation
step.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mission

Enable deterministic, auditable and scalable compliance verification across
the complete software lifecycle while minimizing operational overhead and
maintaining engineering productivity.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Compliance Rules

Rule 01

Every governed activity shall produce compliance evidence.

Rule 02

Only approved policies shall define compliance requirements.

Rule 03

Compliance evaluations shall be reproducible.

Rule 04

Violations shall trigger remediation workflows.

Rule 05

Compliance records shall remain immutable.
